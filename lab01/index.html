Ćwiczenie 1: Artykuł

The pianist who never played

Ian Li

Her deft fingers tapped silently in the air. They had been designed to play melodies, despite never having met a piano.
How could they know this other pianist played better, if theyd never even heard her perform?

The shed door creaked open and the butler walked in.
His warm hands opened up the battery compartment on her back to swap out her fizzling battery.
He turned to walk out, but stopped short when his eyes met her forlorn gaze.

“Its not your fault — your arrival was delayed due to a shipping disruption, and he got so impatient,
he bought a newer model at the local store… Dont be like that. Theres still hope.”
